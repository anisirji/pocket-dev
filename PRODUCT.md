# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac owners who control their own computer from a phone browser. The Mac-only admin page manages phone access, devices, permissions, and service settings. Users connect their phone on the same Wi-Fi and need quick, clear ways to share an address or log in.

## Product Purpose

Pocket Dev exposes terminals, coding chats, Git, files, open apps, and power controls through a phone-friendly browser interface. The admin page makes connection state and access decisions visible on the Mac.

## Brand Personality

Compact, practical, direct. Keep the existing dark interface and familiar controls, as confirmed for the Share & login feature.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative redesigns, marketing-style screens, and extra dialogs for simple sharing actions. Preserve the existing admin visual language.

## Design Principles

- Put connection and onboarding actions where Mac owners can find them quickly.
- Explain the difference between sharing an address and granting a one-time login.
- Reflect current network and access state instead of presenting unusable codes.
- Reuse existing controls, typography, spacing, and status patterns.
- Keep actions and feedback concise and explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard-accessible native controls and visible focus indicators. Give QR images useful accessible names, offer selectable URLs and copy actions as alternatives to scanning, and announce loading errors and expiry changes. Keep the layout usable at narrow widths without relying on color alone.
