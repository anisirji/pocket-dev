# Pocket Dev founder recording

Prepared 18 November 2026. The launch approach now uses Ani's own voice and a real phone workflow. This script is for channels that allow assisted promotional copy. It is not a Hacker News post or comment; HN requires the person to write and post their own text.

## First recording: 45–60 seconds

Record two separate clips. They do not need to be one perfect take.

1. **Face to camera, 10–15 seconds.** Say why you built it, in your own words.
2. **Actual phone demo, 25–35 seconds.** Show one useful task: inspect a terminal command's result or a Git diff while the work stays on your computer. Show the phone and computer together briefly so the connection is clear.
3. **Face to camera, 5–10 seconds.** Invite people to try it and tell you what to improve.

Use a demo project. Keep real passwords and login QR codes out of the recording. Keep the computer on and awake, and show the connection method you actually configured.

## Optional words to adapt

> I kept going back to my desk just to check on coding work. I wanted to spend more time living without losing touch with my work, so I built Pocket Dev.
>
> This is my computer, and this is my phone. I can check my terminal, review a Git change, or continue a coding conversation here. The work still runs on my computer.
>
> It's free and open source. You can copy a setup prompt and let a coding assistant on your computer help install it. Try it and tell me what you'd want to use from your phone.

Use your natural language and voice; Hindi-English is fine. Treat this as talking points, not a speech to memorize. If showing a current coding conversation would reveal private content, show a terminal or Git task in the demo project instead.

## Recording setup

- Your phone camera is enough. Keep it steady, face a window and choose a quiet room.
- Record a short sound check first. Clear speech matters more than background music.
- A landscape talking clip with your face centered can be used with the wide demo. If convenient, record the same short intro vertically too for Reels and Shorts.
- Record the real phone screen separately so its text remains readable in the edit.

## Distribution

- **Reddit / r/SideProject:** one useful demonstration, concise maker context, source link and a specific feedback question. First post waits for the founder recording.
- **X:** up to one distinct post per day for the first week: founder intro, terminal use, Git review, phone setup, computer/phone handoff, a genuine limitation, and an actual improvement or lesson. Reuse footage with a different useful angle.
- **LinkedIn:** three posts across the first week: founder story and demo, a practical workflow, then a real lesson or improvement.
- **Instagram and YouTube:** keep the existing schedule; the founder introduction can become an additional clearly identified video once recorded.
- **Hacker News:** personal writing and manual submission by Ani, per its guidelines. Use the repository and factual product material as references, not generated posting copy.
- **DEV:** an original technical article written by Ani; do not publish AI-generated promotional content under DEV's AI-assisted article policy.
- **Product Hunt:** prepare the listing using the existing logo, real screenshots and public demo. Add the founder recording before choosing the dedicated launch day.
