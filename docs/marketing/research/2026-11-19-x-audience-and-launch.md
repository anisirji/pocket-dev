# Pocket Dev on X: audience research and launch

Checked November 19, 2026 using signed-in public X pages, the existing audience evidence bank, and X's published policies. Observations identify relevant conversations; they do not establish demand for Pocket Dev or forecast views.

## Who the product fits

The strongest audience is developers who already run Claude Code or Codex on their own computer and want to check progress, handle supported approvals, inspect Git changes, or use terminals from their phone. The initial practical audience is macOS users; a host computer, accounts for the coding tools, and network access remain necessary. Adjacent audiences include independent developers maintaining client websites and self-hosters already using SSH/tmux/remote-access tools.

The [earlier audience research](2026-11-18-audience-and-posting-plan.md) records firsthand needs and current workarounds. Those sources remain problem evidence, not Pocket Dev customer evidence. No claim is made that all IDE sessions or operating-system permission dialogs can be controlled remotely.

## Mentions, tags, and reach

Use one relevant hashtag where useful, such as #ClaudeCode for a coding-agent workflow or #OpenSource for a source/setup post. Story posts can use #DeveloperLife. Keep technical terms in ordinary text, attach native video, and link directly to the source or current setup page. Captions use ordinary first-person language and checked claims; fictional episodes and generated narration are disclosed.

Do not mass-tag famous builders or tool teams. A relevant mention of @claude_code could be considered for a specific project conversation or invitation, but the launch uses no account mentions. No unsolicited DMs, automated replies, likes, follows, fabricated engagement, or cross-account boosting were performed.

[X's organic guidance](https://business.x.com/en/basics/get-your-business-started-with-x) recommends conversational, concise text, media, and at most one or two hashtags. [Its authenticity policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity) prohibits bulk unsolicited mentions, irrelevant hashtag manipulation, and duplicative campaigns across multiple accounts. Localization into another language is an allowed distinction, but a suspended account cannot be used as a publishing destination.

Native video and a specific practical situation are creative hypotheses. We have no account analytics establishing a best hour, ideal series, or expected views. The initial 8 PM IST daily slot is a test. Review actual impressions, video playback, replies and useful product questions before changing the next campaign. Do not mistake a view count for adoption.
